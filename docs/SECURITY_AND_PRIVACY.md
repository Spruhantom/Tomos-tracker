# Security and privacy baseline

Child-location privacy and safety are release blockers.

- No covert mode, stealth icon, hidden tracking, silent pairing or bypass of OS permission prompts.
- Child-facing UI clearly indicates whether sharing is active and who can see it.
- Pairing uses short-lived, single-use QR challenges; both devices confirm identity.
- Device keys are unique and revocable. Server authorizes every location read.
- Encrypt transport and storage; protect backups and secrets.
- Rate-limit login, pairing, SOS and location writes; detect replay and stale timestamps.
- Audit access and permission changes without logging precise coordinates.
- Provide export, correction, deletion and retention controls.
- History is disabled by default and expires automatically.
- SOS is not a replacement for emergency services; delivery cannot be guaranteed.

## UK launch gates
Complete a DPIA and specialist UK privacy/legal review. Assess UK GDPR, the ICO Children's Code, age-appropriate transparency, lawful basis, parental authority and children's rights. Complete Google Play background-location disclosures and operational incident response before any child pilot.
